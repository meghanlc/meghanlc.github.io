Getting Started With Running

Running is an activity that can be enjoyed by anyone no matter the experience level. Whether someone is just starting out or wants to improve their current routine, setting realistic goals can help make running a consistent habit.

Preparing for a Run

Preparing for a run includes choosing comfortable clothing and the correct running shoes, planning a route, staying hydrated, eating enough carbs beforehand, and giving yourself enough time to warm up. Starting at a comfortable pace can also help make running more enjoyable, this pace is called a coversational pace.

Staying Consistent

Building a running routine takes time. Creating a realistic schedule and setting small personal goals can help make it easier to stay consistent. It is also important to listen to your body and give yourself time to rest, and to eat enough food to give your body energy.

Setting Personal Goals

Personal running goals can be different for everyone. Some people may want to run a certain distance, improve their endurance and stamina, participate in an event like a race, or simply make running part of their regular routine for overall health.
